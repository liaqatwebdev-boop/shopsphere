          About ShopSphere

          ShopSphere is a demonstration storefront designed to show how a modern online shop can look and behave, from browsing and filtering to cart and checkout, using only HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

          Designed and developed by Liaqat Alikhan, WordPress Web Designer & Developer.

          This is a demo

          • All products, prices, ratings and reviews are fictional sample data.
          • There is no backend, no accounts and no real payments.
          • Orders placed in the checkout are simulated only.
